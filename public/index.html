Methodology & scope

What this prototype can and cannot say

Dataset

UCI Student Performance: Portuguese language course, 649 records collected from two Portuguese secondary schools. The source includes demographics, family context, study habits, absences and final grade (G3).

Target and bands

G3 is grouped into Poor (0�9), Average (10�11), Good (12�15) and Excellent (16�20). Earlier period grades G1 and G2 are excluded from input features to avoid target leakage.

Training approach

A JavaScript CART random forest trained with a fixed seed and stratified 80/20 holdout. Missing values are handled with training-set medians for numeric fields and learned category mappings for categorical features.

Limitations

This historical sample is small, school-specific and not representative of every institution. Association is not causation; confidence shows tree vote agreement, not a calibrated probability. Never use estimates to label or penalize a student.

Source: Cortez & Silva, UCI Machine Learning Repository.
